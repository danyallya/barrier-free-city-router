# Product brief

Problem: Choose routes from recently confirmed reports and expire stale observations.

Audience: people with mobility needs, carers and accessibility groups

## First release

One user role, one core record (route-segments) and one reversible workflow. Delay integrations and notifications until a backlog item justifies them.

## Guardrail

Never promise a route is accessible without fresh evidence; show uncertainty and source time.

Measure task completion, correction rate, offline recovery, trust, retention and deletion only after purpose and consent are documented.
